FAQ

Your questions.

What is DAYSTATE?+

A collection of rituals in drops and sticks for different moments of the day. We are preparing our launch in Colombia. INVIMA registration: [RSA pendiente – por producto].

How do I prepare it?+

Shake and dilute the drops: 2.5 mL in 250 mL of water for MORNING COMPLEX, or in 200 mL for NIGHT COMPLEX and HAIR & SKIN GLOW. BLOOM: one stick in 200 mL of cold water. Do not consume the concentrate undiluted.

Which flavors can I find?+

Lemon and ginger; chamomile and passion fruit; red berries and raspberry; dragon fruit and passion fruit. Each product page shows its flavor and preparation.

Why glass and a carton?+

Glass lets you see the liquid. Keep the bottle in its carton, protected from light. Refer to each product’s storage instructions.

Can I buy now?+

We are still at the waitlist stage. You can request launch news. Prices and the opening date will be announced when confirmed.

What does “Coming soon” mean?+

These products are in development. You can request a notification when they become available.
